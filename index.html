Our Story

A Neighborhood Chair On Clairemont Mesa

SD Barber Shop is a neighborhood barbershop on Clairemont Mesa Blvd in Kearny Mesa, offering men's haircuts, fades, tapers, beard trims, head shaves, and hot towel shaves. Walk-ins are welcome, the shop is wheelchair accessible, and credit cards are accepted. Open six days a week, including Sunday mornings and closed Wednesdays, the shop has built a solid local following, with focused experience cutting Asian hair textures alongside classic fades and tapers.
