SDBarber Shop

Walk-in fades, tapers, beard trims, and shaves in Suite Q on Clairemont Mesa Blvd. Closed Wednesdays.

What they cut

  • Military
  • Fade
  • Taper
  • Regular
  • Beard trim
  • Head shave
  • Hot towel shave
  • Specialty cutsAsian hair

The first four are lettered on the front window: military, fade, taper, reg. Walk-ins are welcome, the shop takes credit cards, and it is wheelchair accessible.

A board on the wall has also listed kids' and seniors' cuts, edge-ups, goatee trims, designs, and straight-razor shaves. Prices are not posted here, so ask when you call (858) 279-8199.

Asian hair specialty cuts

SD Barber Shop lists Asian hair specialty cuts alongside its fades and tapers. Walk in and tell the barber what you want.
